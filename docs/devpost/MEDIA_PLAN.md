## Video demo structure

If you are recording a short demo, use this sequence:

1. Start on the map and explain the problem in one sentence.
2. Open Top Care Gaps and click a high-risk district.
3. Show provider-claim cards and the call/verify next step.
4. Open the provider-trust hover text to explain confidence and provisional uncertainty.
5. End in Top Care Gaps or Copilot to show the planner handoff.
